DC Circuit with Internal Resistance

A real cell is not an ideal EMF. Inside the dashed box, E drives charge while internal resistance r drops voltage. Terminal voltage is V = E − Ir, and green particles show the current I = E / (R + r).

HKDSE Formula Focus

$I = \dfrac{E}{R+r}$
$V = E - Ir = IR$
$P = I^{2}R \quad \eta = \dfrac{R}{R+r}$

Terminal voltage falls as current rises. Efficiency is the fraction of EMF that appears across the load.

Key Concepts

  • EMF E is the energy per coulomb supplied by the cell.
  • Internal r is inside the cell; it always wastes I²r heat.
  • Terminal voltage V = E − Ir equals the load voltage IR.
  • Maximum load power when R = r (then η = 50%).
