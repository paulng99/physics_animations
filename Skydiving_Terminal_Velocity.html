HKDSE formula focus

\( R = \tfrac12\,\rho C_d A v^2 \)

\( \Sigma F = mg - R \quad(\text{down}+) \)

\( a = \Sigma F / m \)

\( v_t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2mg}{\rho C_d A}} \)

At terminal velocity, R = mg so ΣF = 0 and a = 0; speed stays nearly constant.

Why heavy ≠ light at vt

Weight grows with mass (mg), but air resistance depends on shape and speed, not mass. A heavier jumper needs a larger R to balance weight, so equilibrium occurs at a higher terminal speed. Without drag, all masses fall with the same g.
