HKDSE Concept Summary

Alpha (α): very short range in air (a few cm), stopped by paper or skin.

Beta (β): medium range and penetration, reduced by aluminium sheet.

Gamma (γ): no charge, very penetrating, best reduced by thick lead/concrete.

In experiments, subtract background count rate before comparing source intensities.

Model Used in This Simulation

Approximate count model:

$$R \approx R_0\left(\frac{5}{d}\right)^2T_{\text{air}}T_{\text{absorber}} + R_{\text{background}}$$

For beta/gamma absorption we use an exponential trend:

$$T_{\text{absorber}} \approx e^{-\mu x}$$

This is a teaching model, tuned for HKDSE-style qualitative understanding.
