Derivation Route

  1. Evaluate work by the applied force: W = Fs (parallel component only).
  2. Use efficiency to get useful output: Wuseful = ηWinput.
  3. Compute average power by dividing work by time.
  4. Estimate speed v = s/t, then Ek = 1/2mv².

HKDSE Answer Quality Checklist

  • • Include units: J, W, m/s.
  • • State whether force is constant and collinear with motion.
  • • Explain where energy loss goes (thermal/sound).
  • • Keep 2-3 significant figures consistently.
