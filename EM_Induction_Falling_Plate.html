Exam Question (HKDSE style)

A rectangular metal plate parallel to the vertical is released from rest inside a uniform magnetic field pointing out of the page. Neglect air resistance. Which of the following statements is correct?

Physics Explained

  • As the plate falls with velocity v, each free electron inside it moves through the field B. The magnetic force F = Qv×B pushes electrons towards side XY, leaving side WZ positively charged.
  • Charge separation stops growing when the internal electric force balances the magnetic force (eE = evB). A voltage ε = Bvw therefore appears across WZ and XY — statement B is correct.
  • There is no closed circuit, and the field is uniform, so the magnetic flux through any loop inside the plate never changes. No current flows — statement A is wrong.
  • With no current, there is no magnetic braking force. The only force is gravity, so the plate falls with acceleration g — statements C and D are wrong.
