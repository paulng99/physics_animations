Why the two spectra match

Atoms have discrete energy levels. A photon is absorbed only if its energy exactly equals a gap ΔE between two levels: ΔE = hc/λ.

Emission: an excited electron drops down and releases a photon of that same ΔE — bright coloured lines on a dark background.

Absorption: a cool gas sits in front of a hot continuous source. Photons with matching λ are removed — dark lines on a rainbow.

That is why every dark Fraunhofer line of an element sits at the identical wavelength as one of its bright emission lines.
