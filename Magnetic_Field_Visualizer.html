Key Concepts

  • Like poles repel; unlike poles attract.
  • Field lines go from N to S outside the magnet and form closed loops.
  • A compass needle aligns with the local field direction.
  • Field strength is stronger where lines are closer together.

Dipole Model

$\vec{B} \propto \dfrac{\vec{m}}{r^3}$ (far field)

Each magnet is modelled as N and S monopoles. The net field is their superposition. Lines never cross; density indicates |B|.
