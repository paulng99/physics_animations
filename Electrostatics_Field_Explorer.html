HKDSE Formula Focus

  • $E = kQ / r^2$ with $k = 8.99\times 10^9$
  • $F = qE$ force on the test charge
  • Net $\vec{E}$ is the vector sum of all source charges.
  • Like charges repel; unlike charges attract.

Coulomb Field

$\vec{E} = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{Q}{r^2}\hat{r}$
